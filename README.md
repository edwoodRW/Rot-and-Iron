# Rot and Iron

## German-Soviet wartime diplomacy

- Germany can offer a victory settlement when its side controls Moscow and the
  Soviet Union has reached 70% surrender progress. Soviet acceptance starts a
  peace conference, which may partition or annex the USSR. Offers expire after
  90 days and may be sent again after 180 days.
- The Berlin Settlement focus requires Soviet agreement. If Moscow refuses,
  Germany can ask again through the wartime negotiations decisions.
- After twelve months of war, Germany can propose a mutual white peace if
  neither side has reached 70% surrender progress. Soviet acceptance starts a
  zero-score peace conference with all relevant participants. The Berlin
  Settlement focus uses the same conference scopes. The stalemate proposal is
  player-initiated; Soviet AI can accept or refuse it.

The peace-conference participant selection, including China if it is fighting a
separate war, needs in-game verification.
